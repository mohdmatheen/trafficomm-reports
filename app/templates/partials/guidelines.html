Before you upload

Clean, clearly named files are processed first time. Takes a minute to check.

{% if collapsible %}{{ icon('chevron-down') }}
{% else %}{% endif %}

1. Name raw reports like this

Client_Platform_Market_Period.xlsx

  • BWA_Meta_UAE_Oct2026.xlsx
  • BWA_TikTok_KSA_Oct2026_clicks.xlsx
  • BWA_Snapchat_UAE_Sep-Oct2026.csv
  • BWA_Meta_UAE-KSA_Oct2026.xlsx

Platform: Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, Google or LinkedIn. Market: one market (UAE), or several joined with a hyphen (UAE-KSA), not commas. Period: the month (Oct2026) or range (Sep-Oct2026). If one platform needs two exports, add a short note at the end (_clicks, _views). We flag names that don’t follow this after upload.

2. Export the raw data

  • Export from the ad platform with a day or month breakdown covering the full reporting period (and the comparison period, if you add one).
  • Keep the platform’s own column headers. Don’t rename, merge or hide columns, and don’t add notes or extra tables inside the data.
  • Include spend, impressions, reach, link clicks and video plays (Meta 3-second, Snapchat and TikTok 2-second).
  • The market comes from campaign names (UAE, KSA, Dubai…). Check campaigns are named with their market.
  • Open each file before uploading: it should have rows, not just a total, and must not be saved with a filter on.
  • Upload each export once. Remove “(1)” or “copy” duplicates.

3. Templates, decks and screenshots

  • Excel template: upload the latest approved version. In a monthly workbook, put the month in each tab or block title (e.g. “Oct Data”) and label market blocks (UAE, KSA). Only the month your exports cover is filled; other months stay as they are.
  • To fill a monthly template, upload one month’s exports per task.
  • Client PowerPoint: upload last period’s deck. One updated deck is produced per file.
  • Screenshots: name them by creative name or slide number (BWA-Static-EN.png, slide 5.png).

4. Applied automatically on every report

  • Snapchat and TikTok spend is read as USD and converted to AED at the rate on this form (default 3.6725), unless the export states its currency.
  • Views: Meta = 3-second video plays, Snapchat = 2-second video plays, TikTok = 2-second video plays.
  • Clicks are always link clicks (Snapchat swipe ups).
  • Slides marked “Update to Kyra”, “Kyra to update” or “to be shared by client” are never changed.
  • Screenshots are placed with a black border, and insights use professional language.
